How to make maggie

Ingredients

  • Maggie noodle
  • Water
  • Extra masala
  • Salt
  • vegetables

Steps

  1. Boil 1 cup of water
  2. Add maggie noodle
  3. Add the maggie masala
  4. Add vegetables and salt
  5. Cook for 2-3 minutes
  6. Serve hot
